# Vehicle Loan Policy

## Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 21 years old.

Applicants should have a stable source of income.

Applicants with good repayment history receive better approval chances.

---

## Credit History

Applicants with good credit history are eligible for higher loan amounts.

Applicants with poor credit history may receive reduced loan eligibility or rejection.

---

## Repayment Capacity

The customer's total EMI should generally not exceed 40% of monthly income.

Loan amount is calculated based on repayment capacity.

---

## Vehicle Recommendation

Recommended vehicles should fit within the customer's approved budget.

Budget = Approved Loan Amount + Down Payment.

---

## Rejection

Loan applications may be rejected because of:

- Poor credit history

- Existing EMI exceeding repayment capacity

- Insufficient income

- Missing mandatory documents

## Next Steps

Applicants whose loan applications are rejected may:

- Improve their credit history.

- Reduce existing EMI obligations.

- Increase down payment.

- Reapply after improving repayment capacity.
